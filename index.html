Copy & paste mode

No API key needed. At each step the app assembles the complete prompt (instructions, case details and the text of every document). Copy it into Claude.ai, Gemini, ChatGPT or your institution's approved AI tool, then paste the AI's reply back here. The app splits the reply into the memo, source check and verification table, and exports the memo to Word.

Scanned PDFs with no selectable text can't be included as text. The app lists any such files so you can attach them in the AI chat yourself.

Drafting instructions

The app drafts from your promotion-memo prompt: source and accuracy rules, analytical expectations, tone, memo structure and the required outputs. You can review or adjust it.

Pre-draft analysis Recommended

Before drafting, the AI reviews every document and prepares an analysis: the conventions in the example memos, the closest example, an evidence inventory, a comparison with the criteria, and any inconsistencies or gaps. It then lists questions where a missing fact or conflict would materially affect the memo. Your answers go into the draft.
